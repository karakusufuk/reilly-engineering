Started with one machine in a rented unit.

In 1998 Declan Reilly set up a second-hand CNC mill in a small unit in Swords, sold it to a toolmaker in Finglas, and stayed on to show him how to run it. That has been the business ever since: sell the right machine, then stay with it.

Today his son Ciarán runs the company. The machines are bigger and the tolerances are tighter, but the phone is still answered in Swords.

The two builders we sell

Aldner

Germany. Family-owned since 1951. Vertical, five-axis and mill-turn machining centres, built heavy and built to be rebuilt.

Hoshida

Japan. Since 1964. Lathes, Swiss-type machines and wire EDM, known for holding a micron for twenty years.
